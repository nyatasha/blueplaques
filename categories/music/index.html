Music

70 of the 1,001 English Heritage blue plaques on this map are classified as Music. The earliest was erected in 1909, the most recent in 2024. They are most concentrated in Westminster, Camden, Kensington and Chelsea and Hampstead. Every name below links to that plaque's page, with its inscription, address and location on the map.
