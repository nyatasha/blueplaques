Architecture

39 of the 1,001 English Heritage blue plaques on this map are classified as Architecture. The earliest was erected in 1910, the most recent in 2024. They are most concentrated in Camden, Hampstead and Westminster. Every name below links to that plaque's page, with its inscription, address and location on the map.
