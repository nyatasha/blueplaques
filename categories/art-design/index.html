Art & Design

133 of the 1,001 English Heritage blue plaques on this map are classified as Art & Design. The earliest was erected in 1885, the most recent in 2025. They are most concentrated in Westminster, Kensington and Chelsea, Camden and Hammersmith and Fulham. Every name below links to that plaque's page, with its inscription, address and location on the map.
