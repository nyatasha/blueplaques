About

London Uncovered

London Uncovered is an interactive map of London's blue plaques and a light exploration game built on top of it. Explore the map, find plaques near you, and collect the discoveries you make. Your progress is saved in this browser — there is no account and nothing to sign up for.

What are blue plaques?

The English Heritage blue plaque scheme has commemorated notable people in London since 1866 and is the oldest scheme of its kind in the world. A plaque marks a building where someone lived or worked. This map covers 935 English Heritage blue plaques across London, from Westminster and Chelsea to Hampstead, Brixton and Bow.

Who you will find

Plaques mark the homes and workplaces of writers, artists, scientists, performers, musicians, architects, campaigners and sportspeople — among them Freddie Mercury, John Lennon, Jimi Hendrix, Charles Darwin, Florence Nightingale, Karl Marx, Oscar Wilde, Alan Turing, Charles Dickens, Virginia Woolf, Ada Lovelace, Mary Seacole and Agatha Christie. Every plaque is sorted into a category — Writers & Literature, Art & Design, Science & Technology, Film & Theatre, Music, Politics & Society, Architecture and Sport — so you can explore London by the kind of story you like.

Privacy

Location is only requested when you tap a location button, is used solely to show discoveries around you, and never leaves your device. No coordinates are stored or sent anywhere. Your collection lives in this browser's local storage.
