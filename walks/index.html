London blue plaques

Self-guided walks

London's blue plaques are not spread evenly: in a few neighbourhoods they stand within a couple of hundred metres of one another, close enough to string into an afternoon. These four walks do that. Each one lists its stops in walking order with the distance between them, and every stop links to that plaque's page and its place on the map.
